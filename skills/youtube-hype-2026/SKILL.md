# YouTube Hype + Shorts 2026 (and YPP changes coming Feb 2027)

Researched 2026-11-23. Official sources first; secondary claims are labelled.

## 1. Hype — the free discovery boost for small creators

**What it is:** viewers "hype" a new long-form video, giving it points.
Videos with the most points each week appear on a country-specific
leaderboard (not personalised — every viewer in a country sees the same
list), which drives browse-feed discovery. Announced at Made on YouTube
Sep 2024; by Made on YouTube Sep 23, 2026 YouTube reported 1 billion+
hypes across 75M+ videos.

**Rules (YouTube Help, "Hype a video", answer/15333273):**

- Channel must be in the YouTube Partner Program with 500–500,000
  subscribers. Grow past 500k and you lose access.
- Long-form videos only — Shorts CANNOT be hyped. Only videos uploaded
  within the last 7 days. Unlisted/private, made-for-kids, age-restricted,
  copyright-claimed videos and live streams/Premieres while live are
  excluded.
- Each viewer gets 3 free hypes per week (refresh Monday 00:00 local
  time); can put all 3 on one video or spread them. A hype cannot be
  undone.
- Small-creator bonus: points scale inversely with subscriber count.
  1 hype on a 500-sub channel = 7,500 points; on a 500,000-sub channel =
  50 points (150x difference).
- Hype is enabled by default for eligible creators; toggle in YouTube
  Studio → Settings → Channel → Advanced settings ("Let viewers hype my
  content").
- Hype does NOT influence search/recommendations directly (YouTube's own
  statement at launch); its value is the leaderboard + badge visibility.
- 39 countries have free Hype, including the US, UK, India, Indonesia.
  Bangladesh is NOT on the list — but the viewer's country is what
  matters, so a US/UK audience can hype your videos. The creator's own
  location is not the gate; YPP membership + the 500–500k band is.

**Paid Hype (Jewels → Rubies) — expanded November 2026:**

- Viewers who used their 3 free hypes can buy more with Jewels (YouTube's
  in-app currency); the creator earns Rubies, paid out via AdSense
  alongside other earnings. Paying fans' handles appear in a recognition
  section in the video description for the first 7 days.
- Paid Hype launched in Brazil, South Korea, Taiwan, Japan earlier in
  2026; expanded early November 2026 to 9 more markets: US, Canada,
  Germany, Ireland, Italy, Poland, Spain, Turkey, UK (13 paid markets
  total). Source: TeamYouTube pinned post (JJ) on YouTube Help Community,
  reported by PPC Land, Nov 2026.
- To receive paid hype earnings a creator must sign the Virtual Items
  Module, complete identity verification, and have "Let viewers hype" on.
- UNVERIFIED — do not quote as fact: YouTube has published NO price per
  hype in Jewels, NO Jewels→Rubies conversion rate for hype, and NO
  revenue share. (For live-stream gifts the ratio was 1 Ruby per 2 Jewels,
  1 Ruby = US$0.01 — that is gifts, not hype.) Jewels buyers must be 18+;
  Jewels are non-refundable.

## 2. Shorts algorithm: original content wins (Oct 1, 2026)

TeamYouTube announcement, Oct 1, 2026 ("Prioritizing original content on
Shorts", support.google.com/youtube/thread/470890423):

- Shorts recommendation systems now prioritise original content and reduce
  the reach of content re-uploaded from other creators "without adding
  anything of your own." Channels "primarily aggregating or re-uploading"
  get less Shorts-feed distribution. Judged at channel level ("primarily").
- Does NOT count as original: minor technical edits or template-based
  changes — crops, flips, speed changes, filters, transcribed captions,
  and (per Creator Liaison Rene Ritchie, Creator Insider) a voiceover
  that only narrates what is on screen.
- Does count: your own original commentary, unique edits, storytelling,
  distinct point of view, or footage you filmed yourself.
- Recovery: reach is re-evaluated when a channel shifts to original
  content — switching is not a permanent penalty.
- Using your OWN long-form clips as Shorts is not penalised (YouTube Help,
  clips/Shorts page: "You won't be penalized for using your content in a
  video clip or Short").
- Related autumn-2026 Shorts features: Shorts series (seasons/episodes,
  rolling out since Sep 23, 2026); Studio clips tool now publishes
  vertical Shorts cut from your long-form videos (live Nov 12, 2026,
  AI-suggested clips in 10 countries/7 languages); members-only Shorts
  series episodes with a join-to-keep-watching flow (Oct 27, 2026).
- Shorts bonus pilot (Oct 19, 2026): invite-only monthly bonus on engaged
  Shorts views for creators uploading a minimum number of public Shorts —
  secondary-source only (markstudios), no application; watch for an
  invite, do not chase it.

## 3. YPP monetization bar DOUBLES Feb 1, 2027 (announced Aug 10, 2026)

First major YPP overhaul since 2018. Corroborated by TechTimes, Hypebeast,
Metricool, AIR Media-Tech and CreatiCalc, all citing YouTube's official
Partner Program changes page.

| | Until Jan 31, 2027 | From Feb 1, 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Join YPP (ad + Premium revenue) | 1,000 subs + 4,000 qualified watch hours / 12 mo, OR 10M qualified Shorts views / 90 days | 1,000 subs + 8,000 qualified watch hours / 365 days, OR 20M qualified Shorts views / 90 days |
| Applies to | everyone | new applicants only — existing partners grandfathered |
| Earn from Shorts Creator Pool (monthly) | no ongoing view floor | must maintain 10M qualified Shorts views / rolling 90 days, checked monthly |
| Fan-funding tier (500 subs) | 500 subs + 3,000 hrs or 3M Shorts views | UNCHANGED — this is the Hype-eligible tier |
| Terms | current modules | accept updated Watch Page / Shorts / Commerce modules in Studio by Jan 31, 2027 |

- An application reaching YouTube by 23:59 UTC Jan 31, 2027 is judged
  against the OLD 4,000-hour bar even if decided later (per AIR
  Media-Tech, an official YouTube partner). A rejection in January means
  reapplying at 8,000 hours.
- 20M Shorts views / 90 days ≈ 222,000 qualified views/day.
- Also from Feb 2027: targeted Shorts ads begin (per YouTube help docs).

## What this means for a small Shorts channel

1. **Hype is a 500-subscriber goal, not a today tool.** The realistic
   path: reach 500 subs + 3,000 watch hours (or 3M Shorts views) →
   fan-funding tier → Hype unlocks for long-form videos. At 500 subs each
   hype is worth the maximum 7,500 points — the bonus is designed exactly
   for channels at that size.
2. **Hype does NOT help Shorts.** Only long-form videos in their first 7
   days can be hyped. A long-form catalogue is the hypeable format;
   Shorts remain the discovery funnel that feeds it.
3. **The Oct 1 originality change rewards real commentary and punishes
   lazy reuse.** Real gameplay + your own narration is the right model —
   but narration that merely describes the action does NOT count as
   original; the commentary/analysis/storytelling must be the point.
   Fully original Shorts (own script, own visuals) benefit.
4. **The Feb 2027 bar makes the long-form route more valuable.** 20M
   Shorts views in 90 days is a steep bar for a small channel; 8,000 watch
   hours from long-form is the more reachable path — another reason to
   build the long-form catalogue now, while the old 4,000-hour bar still
   applies (until Jan 31, 2027).
5. **Paid Hype is future income, not current income.** Once a channel
   hits 500 subs + YPP, fans in paid-Hype markets (including the US)
   could pay to hype long-form videos. Do not quote any $ amount until
   YouTube publishes the Jewel price / Ruby rate.
6. **First-week push matters more now.** Hype's 7-day window + the Shorts
   originality push both reward a strong launch week: ask the audience to
   hype in the first days (once eligible), and front-load promotion.
